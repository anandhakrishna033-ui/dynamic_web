Our Story

From a small kitchen to Trivandrum's favorite artisanal bakery.

The Pursuit of the Perfect Crumb

Daily Crumbs started with a simple obsession: recreating the perfect, blistered sourdough crust and the impossibly flaky layers of a classic Parisian croissant right here in Kerala.

We believe that baking is a science, but feeding people is an art. We refuse to use commercial yeast or artificial dough conditioners. Instead, we rely on time, temperature, and our wild yeast starter to do the heavy lifting.

Every loaf is hand-shaped, every pastry is hand-laminated, and every customer is treated like family.
